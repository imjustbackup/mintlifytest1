LEMON Manuals MCP is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that gives your AI assistant searchable, read-only access to the LEMON and CHARM car repair manual archives — covering roughly 305,000 vehicles from 1960 through 2025. Once registered, your AI client can find vehicles, read manual pages, search full-text content, and retrieve diagrams, all without leaving the chat interface.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register the MCP server with your AI client">
    Point your MCP-compatible AI client (such as Claude) at your running LEMON Manuals MCP server. This one-time step makes all five tools available in every future conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for a vehicle">
    Ask your AI assistant to find a vehicle by make, model, year, and engine. The AI calls `search_vehicles` against the local index and returns matching drivetrains with their manual paths.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the manual">
    Ask your AI assistant to open a specific section — for example, the brake system repair procedure. The AI follows the path returned in the previous step, fetches the page, and presents the content as readable text.
  </Step>
</Steps>
